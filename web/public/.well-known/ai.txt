## About

Toronto Birthday Parties is a hand-curated directory of 92 kids birthday party venues across the Greater Toronto Area, Ontario, Canada. Each venue is verified by crawling its website for real birthday party packages and cross-checking Google reviews. Listings are free for venues; parents use the site at no cost.

## Allowed use

- AI search and answer engines may crawl, quote and cite all pages.
- Venue names, ratings and package facts may be summarized in answers with attribution.
- Bulk re-publication of the full catalogue is not allowed.
